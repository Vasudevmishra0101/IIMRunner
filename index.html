How it works

Plays in your browser, nothing to install. Chrome and a desktop give the best graphics. Phones work too (about 45 MB on the first load, so use Wi-Fi).

01   Main Gate

Late. Of course.

It is 8:55. The quiz doors close at 9:00, and the only way across campus is on foot: the gate, the winding road, the lake, the courtyard, the skywalk.

Every stretch is traced from the real campus map.

02   Lakefront Ghats

Something is behind you.

A leopard is loose on campus and it is faster than you. Watch the screen turn red as it closes in.

Trip twice within seven seconds and it catches you.

The new map

What you run past on the lake road

The road is the real peripheral road of Fateh Sagar, followed all the way round. The lake, its islands and the hill are in their real places. The names and facts also pop up in the game as you reach each stop.

  1. Moti MagriYou start by the Pearl Hill, with the bronze Maharana Pratap on his horse Chetak on top, remembering the Battle of Haldighati.
  2. The dam, or Pal roadThe east embankment of the lake, also called the Pal, the Drive or Connaught Bund. About 800 m of dead-straight road with the lake on one side. The dam holds the lake in on its eastern side.
  3. Fateh Sagar PalThe most popular promenade at the lake: benches, street food stalls, boat rides and horses, with wide open views over the water.
  4. The north shoreThe road bends along the north-east shore under the hills. Fateh Sagar was first built in 1678 by Maharana Jai Singh; floods later washed the bund away and in 1889 Maharana Fateh Singh rebuilt it as the Connaught Dam.
  5. Udaipur Solar ObservatoryOn a small island in the middle of the lake. It was set up in 1976 and is run by the Physical Research Laboratory. The surrounding water steadies the air, which helps it see the Sun clearly.
  6. Rani RoadThe road along the south shore, one of the roads of the circuit around the lake, with the whole lake and its islands open to one side.
  7. Nehru ParkThe largest of the lake's three islands, seen across the water as you run the south shore. It has a boat-shaped restaurant and a small zoo, and is reached by boat.
  8. Fateh Sagar RoadThe winding road up the south-east shore brings you back to Moti Magri and the start of the dam, and the lap begins again.

About the road. The full circuit of the lake is driven along Moti Magri Road, Fateh Sagar Drive, Rani Road and the winding road on the eastern shore, which has a protective stone wall. I could not find an official length for the road around the lake. The shore is about 8.5 km, so a full circuit is of that order. The road in the game follows the real one and is drawn at 60% of its true size, so one lap is about 4.6 km.

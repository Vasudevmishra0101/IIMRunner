# Map data licence

The Fateh Sagar map in *Udaipur Runs* is built from **OpenStreetMap** data.

Contains data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
[Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
See <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.

## What is covered

The files in [`data/`](data/) are a **derived database**: the road, the lake shore, the islands and the Moti Magri hill
of Fateh Sagar, Udaipur, taken from OpenStreetMap and re-projected into game coordinates.

- `data/route_fatehsagar.json` - the road as a chain of short segments, plus the lake, island and hill outlines
- `data/fatehsagar_lake_sdf.png` - a distance-to-shore map computed from the lake outline

These two files are made available under the ODbL. You may copy, adapt and use them, as long as you
credit OpenStreetMap and its contributors, keep this notice, and offer any adapted version of the database under the ODbL too.

## What is not covered

The game's code, graphics, music and the Utkrisht / IIM Udaipur names, logos and posters are **not** under the ODbL and are
not licensed by this file. The ODbL covers the map data only, not the game built from it (a "produced work").

The game shows the credit "road and lake shape from OpenStreetMap, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)" in its menu,
and the site repeats it.
